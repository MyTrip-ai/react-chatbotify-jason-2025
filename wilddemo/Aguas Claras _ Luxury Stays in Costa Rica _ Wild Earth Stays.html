Welcome to the Jungle! A soulful Caribbean retreat where art, nature, and luxury inspire deep connection and renewal.

An intimate 12-room luxury retreat blending art, nature, and comfort. With direct access to Playa Chiquita, its curated bungalows and suites feature original artworks and handcrafted furnishings, reflecting the region’s vibrant culture. Whether you seek relaxation or adventure, this eco-luxury hideaway offers an inspiring connection to Costa Rica’s Caribbean soul. The hotel's commitment to sustainability is evident through its eco-friendly practices and community engagement initiatives.

Adorned by floral water colour paintings to mosaics, this modern bohemian hideaway is for discerning eco-travelers, who seek a delightful eclectic retreat while surrounded by the natural Caribbean beauty.

Hidden along Playa Chiquita, Hotel Aguas Claras is an intimate eco-retreat where art, nature, and sustainability come together. As part of the Cayuga Collection, this beachfront sanctuary offers uniquely designed bungalows and suites, lush gardens, and direct beach access—inviting you to immerse yourself in Costa Rica’s wild beauty.

Features only at
Aguas Claras
Local Culture

Enjoy a cacao ceremony as part of the Hotel’s wellness offering. Or better yet, meet the indigenous BriBrí group in the mountains of the Watsi Reservation to experience a full, local cacao tour.

Dining

Enjoy an array of colorful, local, organic, beach eats and cocktails on the nicest beaches of the Caribbean coast – uniquely with an Afro-Caribbean twist!

Wild Life

When going on a hike in one of the many nature reserves, see flashes of rainbow fly through the lush jungle atmosphere. You may even spot a sloth!

Bohemian Art Gardens

Grab a book or a friend and stroll through the lush, tropical gardens. Unique with Victorian fountains and original, local art – all blending harmoniously in with the bohemian themed interior.

Beach Days

Chill by the ocean and enjoy complimentary chairs and a bike to get you there
